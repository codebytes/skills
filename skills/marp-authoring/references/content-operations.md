# Content Operations

Treat a Marp slide as a complete block: local directives, visible content,
embedded HTML, and trailing speaker-note comments.

## Add

1. Identify the narrative gap.
2. Insert the slide after the setup and before the first content that depends on it.
3. Reuse an existing slide pattern.
4. Update agendas, section counts, and cross-references when present.

## Split

- Keep the premise or comparison setup on the first slide.
- Move evidence, examples, or implementation detail to the second.
- Repeat enough context that the second slide is understandable.
- Move detailed explanation into notes instead of duplicating it visibly.

## Merge

- Keep the strongest title and conclusion.
- Remove repeated setup.
- Preserve citations and notes from both slides.
- Do not merge if the result has more than one primary idea.

## Reorder

- Move the entire slide block, including directives immediately before the title
  and notes immediately after the visible content.
- Check transitions such as "as we just saw" and update them.
- Recheck section dividers and agenda order.

## Rewrite or condense

- Preserve factual meaning, qualification, dates, units, and source attribution.
- Prefer short declarative statements.
- Move useful detail to notes rather than deleting it.
- Report intentionally removed content when there is no appropriate note.

## Delete

- Confirm that no later slide depends on the deleted definition or example.
- Remove stale agenda entries and links.
- Preserve reusable assets unless the user also asked for cleanup.
