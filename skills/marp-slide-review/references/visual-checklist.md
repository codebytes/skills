# Visual Review Checklist

Inspect every rendered slide at a readable size.

- No text or media crosses the slide boundary.
- Titles do not wrap at awkward words or collide with decoration.
- Body text is readable without zooming.
- Columns, cards, and repeated elements align consistently.
- Gaps are intentional and balanced.
- Images are sharp, correctly cropped, and large enough to understand.
- Charts include units, labels, sources, and accessible colors.
- Diagram labels remain readable at presentation distance.
- Code is limited to the lines needed for the point.
- Tables are not too dense for the projected size.
- Headers, footers, and page numbers do not collide with content.
- Links and citations remain visible.
- No placeholder text, broken images, or runtime rendering errors remain.
- The visual hierarchy makes the intended conclusion obvious.

Report findings as:

```text
Slide 7 - warning - comparison labels are too small at projected size.
Suggested fix: split the comparison or shorten each label.
```
