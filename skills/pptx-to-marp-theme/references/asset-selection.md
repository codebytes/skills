# Asset Selection

The extractor inventories image media and scores likely logos based on:

- Use in a slide master
- Use in one or more layouts
- Repetition across slides
- Small area relative to the slide
- Placement near a slide edge
- Transparent PNG content

This score is only a heuristic.

## Usually keep

- Logos and identity marks
- Repeated title or section backgrounds
- Footer strips and decorative motifs
- Small reusable icons owned by the organization
- Browser-compatible PNG, JPEG, GIF, SVG, or WebP assets

## Usually exclude

- Stock photos
- One-off screenshots
- Large slide-specific photography
- Duplicate resized copies
- Images with unclear reuse rights
- EMF and WMF files that cannot render reliably in browsers

Review SVG files before use because SVG can contain active content or external
references. Optimize large raster images and preserve enough resolution for
full-screen display.
